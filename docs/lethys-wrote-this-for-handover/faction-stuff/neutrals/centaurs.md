## The tribe
- **Role:** centaur clans of the open plains, guarding their sacred grounds.
- **How it plays:** the Herd tests **your melee and your turtling**. Their archers are hard to pin down with blades,
  their kick breaks a defending unit's guard, their seer turns a killing blow aside, and their khan rides down a whole
  front line. A squad of slow melee that defends a lot struggles; ranged and spells do well.
- **Tiers:** four tier-1 units and a tier-2 khan.

## Tier 1
### Outrider
- **Role:** ranged skirmisher.
- **Stats:** health low · damage low (ranged) · armor none · initiative high.
- **Abilities:** *Skirmish* (passive): melee hits on it deal half; it never stands still long enough.

### Lancer
- **Role:** melee that runs through.
- **Stats:** health medium · damage medium · armor low · initiative medium.
- **Abilities:** *Run through* (its attack): hits its target and, for half, the enemy directly behind it.

### Hoofbreaker
- **Role:** anti-turtle melee.
- **Stats:** health medium-high · damage medium · armor low · initiative medium.
- **Abilities:** *Rear and kick* (passive): against a defending unit it deals more and breaks the defence.

### Starseer
- **Role:** support.
- **Stats:** health low · damage very low (ranged) · armor none · initiative medium.
- **Abilities:** *Read the stars* (main action): an ally is warned: the next hit on it misses entirely.

## Tier 2
### Herd-Khan
- **Role:** leader.
- **Stats:** health high · damage medium-high · armor low · initiative high.
- **Abilities:** *Thundering charge* (once per battle, main action): rides down the enemy front line, hitting every
  unit in it.

## Camps (Claude)
Weak: two Outriders, Lancer, Starseer. Medium: Hoofbreaker, Lancer, two Outriders, Starseer (level 2). Strong:
Herd-Khan, Hoofbreaker, Lancer, two Outriders, Starseer (level 4).

## Look (Claude)
Not noble Greek centaurs: weathered horse-folk of the steppe, braids and bone beads, painted flanks, horse-hide
cloaks; the outrider with a recurve bow and a quiver of fletched javelins; the lancer barded in scale; the hoofbreaker
heavy-hoofed with iron-shod feet; the starseer old, blindfolded, star charts tattooed down her flanks; the khan with a
mane of trophies and a long lance.

**Status:** in game as `outrider`, `lancer`, `hoofbreaker`, `starseer`, `herd_khan` (`?fight=herd`); not on the map.

## Concept log (Claude; every image in `art/candidates/units/neutrals/herd/`)
**Round one (2026-10-11):** centaurs came out well bodied. The blindfolded starseer with star charts tattooed down her
grey flank is the standout; the antlered one with hanging discs, the khan in a horse-skull helm, the painted lancer
with red handprints, and the woman outrider with ochre stripes read apart. Misses: the dusty outrider came out as a
rider on a horse, the scarred hoofbreaker with a horse's head on a human torso, and most wear saddles, which centaurs
wouldn't. **Round two:** those two again, the body named first and "no saddle".
**Round two (2026-10-12):** both are centaurs now (a human torso on a horse's body, no rider). The dusty outrider
still has a saddle, and the hoofbreaker came out as a woman rather than a scarred brute: judge them as they are, or
I go again.
**Round four (2026-10-12; the user: "some of this art is weird"):** saddles were the weird part (a horse missing its
rider), and "no saddle" didn't hold; naming the bare back did. Clean now: the khan (bearded, horse-skull helm, red
cloak, both seeds), the lancer with red handprints (both), the hoofbreaker seed 1001 (bald brute, iron-shod), the
outrider seed 1000 (ochre stripes, quiver). Misses kept aside: hoofbreaker 1000 (a horse's head and saddle again),
outrider 1001 (a rider on a horse).
