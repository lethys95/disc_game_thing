## The tribe
- **Role:** beasts of the forest and hills, guarding lairs and dens; a tribe with no speech, no casters and no healing.
- **How it plays:** the Wild tests **your back row**. Wolves don't respect a front line, a boar's tusks leave wounds
  that keep bleeding, crows blind your strongest hitter. A squad that hides its casters behind a wall of armor is
  exposed; a squad that can take a few bites and hit back hard does well.
- **Tiers:** four tier-1 beasts and a tier-2 alpha for strong lairs.

## Tier 1
### Grey Wolf
- **Role:** fast melee that reaches past the front line.
- **Stats:** health low · damage low · armor none · initiative high.
- **Abilities:** *Pounce* (its attack): may strike any enemy within one column of it, front or back row, as long as
  the wolf stands in its own front line.
- **Strong against / weak against:** casters and archers behind a wall / armored units (it bites for little).

### Boar
- **Role:** melee bruiser.
- **Stats:** health medium-high · damage medium · armor low · initiative medium.
- **Abilities:** *Gore* (passive): the unit it hits bleeds a little at the start of each of its turns, for the rest of
  the battle; more gores add up.
- **Strong against / weak against:** long fights / squads that kill fast or heal.

### Cave Bear
- **Role:** wall.
- **Stats:** health very high · damage medium · armor none · initiative low.
- **Abilities:** *Wounded fury* (passive): below half its health, it hits much harder. Leave it hurt and it mauls.
- **Strong against / weak against:** chip damage / burst that takes it from full to dead.

### Carrion Murder (a swarm of crows)
- **Role:** ranged nuisance.
- **Stats:** health very low · damage very low (ranged) · armor none · initiative high.
- **Abilities:** *Peck the eyes* (its attack): the unit it hits deals less damage until the end of the next round.
- **Strong against / weak against:** one big hitter / many even units.

## Tier 2
### Old Grey (the alpha)
- **Role:** leader, fast melee.
- **Stats:** health high · damage medium-high · armor none · initiative high.
- **Abilities:** *Pounce* as the wolves. *Howl* (once per battle, main action): every ally's next hit deals more.

## Camps (Claude)
Weak: two Grey Wolves, Boar, Carrion Murder. Medium: Cave Bear, two Grey Wolves, Boar, Carrion Murder (level 2).
Strong: Old Grey, Cave Bear, Boar, two Grey Wolves, Carrion Murder (level 4).

## Look (Claude)
Real animals, painted gothic: lean grey wolves with scarred muzzles; a black-bristled boar with yellowed, cracked
tusks; a huge cave bear with mange and old arrowheads in its hide; a churning cloud of carrion crows around a dead
branch; Old Grey, a giant pale wolf with a torn ear and a mane of frost-white fur.

**Status:** in game as `grey_wolf`, `boar`, `cave_bear`, `carrion_murder`, `old_grey` (`?fight=wild`); not on the map.
