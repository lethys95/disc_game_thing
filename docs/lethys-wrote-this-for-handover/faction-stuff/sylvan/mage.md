# Overall

Sylvans' main focus is not the mage line, but it's not ending quickly either. It's about a middle in types of strength, so we'll land on tier 4 as the last step, not continuing to tier 5 most likely.

The main focus will be trying to tie in the theme of what nature does. We're also trying to fit the duality of the circle of life and death. Unlike wastes, sylvans' side of death is more balance, decay and regrowth. 

Mechanic-wise, we're probably going to do a split between disabler and nuke. Our nuke will have some flexibility which also gives <i>some</i> support capabilities. It'll double-edged in a way. I have an idea that it can target both allies and enemies. If targeting allies, it heals, and then puts a dot on for part of the damage done. The opposite would be targeting enemies, dealing damage and then healing back some. The damage side will be more powerful, as to not steal the role of the support. The damage side will be quite intense and bursty, but you basically have to use it correctly, or it can just heal your opponent up again.
