## Shield rules (combining this with `../pillars.md` and the faction notes)
- A shield is a separate pool that absorbs hits before health. When it's down, full damage hits health (pillars).
- It fully regenerates after combat (user; faction notes: "heals quickly after combat").
- Healing never restores it; only shield-restoring effects do (user).
- Faction notes: shields "don't get bonuses from defend". Provisional reading: Defend's halving doesn't apply to damage the shield absorbs. (questions.md)
