Difficulty: hard.

Generally speaking, nexus is about quality over quantity. Units are high in instant damage potential, but costly and quickly falls off.

Losing units is generally painful, and the faction relies on tech, spells, gimmicks to succeed.

Split between casters and automatons. Automatons' health pool consists mostly of a shield, which heals quickly after combat, but is generally fairly compared to health, doesn't get bonuses from defend, etc.

Support units restore shields instead of health, meaning what they get countered fairly easily. No true healing: rechargers restore automaton shields and can't save non-automatons.

Reference: check the "Storm" magic keyword — a lot of combo play, countering enemy plans.

Spells: mostly nuke.
