# Jilliath — The Inquisition

## User's notes
Core identity (user, 2026-09-25): **Sacrifice.**

Mana color: **Red** — "Scarlet Crusade-esque. Humans. Vengeance angels, Fanatics" (Unreal attempt, `Mana.as`, 2024)

Difficulty: medium.

Generally speaking, inquisition's overall strategy is to make playing the game painful for the enemy.

The units are very binary in terms of whether or not they're shock or defensive. A lot to do with health manipulation, using it as a resource.

A lot with ultimatums, putting the enemy in a position, where they must make difficult decisions.

A lot of punishment, counterplay, putting your thumb in the wound, making a bad situation worse.

Fighter line displays dichotomy between faith and fanaticism. Paladin line being about defensives, tanks. Fanatic line being about self-sacrifice, burst, quick victory at a high cost. Full spec: `../units/jilliath-melee-line.md`.

Spells: targeting infrastructure a lot, making it easier to conquer territory.

Healing: most healing of any faction, with side effects (martyrdom).
