# Items (canon)

The user's verdicts on Claude's brainstorm (`brainstorm/items.md`, notes written 2026-09-29), with the user's changes. Numbers are provisional until built. The Ankh, Hatchet and Outlaw's pocketwatch are the user's own (`rules/items.ts`).

## The user's principles (from the notes)
- **No faction-specific items**: "not fun to get unless you're playing the faction", and it scales poorly with more factions. An item may interact with a mechanic, but then every faction must get something from it.
- **No cursed or temporary items** in practice: "people just pick consistent items"; "temporary equipment is frustrating, players skip these".
- **Leadership is the strongest stat**: hand it out sparingly (at most +1), or "a lucky drop will just win you the game".
- **No hidden luck in loot**: "maybe the player should be able to see the loot in dungeons beforehand, to fit our no-RNG theme."
- Avoid mental overhead (alternating states to track) and anything too strong to counter.

## The user's new ideas (same notes)
- **Dungeon loot is visible before the fight** (no-RNG theme): hovering or peeking a dungeon shows what it rewards.
- **Waystone node** (inspired by the Twin compasses): holding a city with a Waystone gives the spell "Way portal"; casting it teleports the leaders elevated in that city to it. If the city is taken, those leaders no longer count as elevated there. Recorded in `nodes.md`.
