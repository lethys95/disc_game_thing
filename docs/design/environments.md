# Environments (user, 2026-10-05)

"We might want to add environments as a mechanic. They'll work a bit like planechase does in that one MTG mode. Biomes
will probably use these too." And on biomes: "we need to tie them [tribes] to biomes… We need more biomes."

(Planechase: a face-up plane card applies a rule to everyone while it's in play, and the game can move on to the next
plane.) How environments should work here (battle or map, when they change, what triggers them) is the user's to
shape; Claude's pitch, when written, goes below, marked.

## Claude's pitch (2026-10-05; for the user to cut, rename or reshape)
**Environments are battle cards, drawn from the biome's deck.** Like a Planechase plane: one is face up for the whole
battle and bends a rule for both sides; at a fixed moment it can give way to the next card ("the weather turns").
- **When:** a battle starts with an environment drawn (seeded, like tarot) from the deck of the biome it's fought in.
  At the start of round 4, the next card turns over: a long fight changes ground halfway. (In Planechase the players
  roll to move on; a fixed round keeps it deterministic and predictable. An item, a spell or a unit could force a turn
  later.)
- **Shown as a card,** in the same fan as tarot: a big card at the battle's start, then a small one in the HUD, both
  sides seeing all of it (unlike tarot, nothing about it is secret).
- **They play with what exists:** the damage types and statuses, initiative, ranged vs melee, corpses, healing.
  Sketches, one rule each:
  - *Downpour:* everyone is wet at every round start (lightning and fire matter more; burning goes out).
  - *Sandstorm:* ranged attacks deal 25% less.
  - *Mire:* everyone has −10 initiative (the fen idea from `brainstorm/biomes.md`).
  - *Thin veil:* the dead go to the graveyard without decay (the haunted woods idea).
  - *Blood moon:* every unit has Crit 5.
  - *Smoke:* every unit has Evasion 4.
  - *Charged air:* shields regenerate twice as fast (the storm scar idea).
  - *Hallowed ground:* healing heals 25% more.
- **Biomes then are:** a look (ground, props, light, weather effects: rain, sand, fog, which the user wants as effects
  later), a tribe (the desert's gnolls), a node pool (questions #6), and an environment deck of a few cards.
- **The lighting angle the user liked:** environments are also the place for light: Night and fog dim the field; the
  Drawn's light-stealing could darken a battle.

**Biomes to add (the brainstorm's list, not canon):** old forest, haunted woods, fen, highlands, salt flats, storm scar,
pilgrim's plains, beside the temperate land and the desert. Tribe fits: the Drawn in the haunted woods (dark, things
drawn to light); the carnival on the roads (pilgrim's plains), or roaming; trolls (the user's earlier idea) in the
highlands; bandits stay in the temperate land. Each new biome costs ground textures and a handful of props (TRELLIS),
like the desert did.
