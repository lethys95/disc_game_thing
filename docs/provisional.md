- **#66** **Tarot (2026-10-05; the user's mechanic, Claude's tasks, rewards and numbers).** *Tarot x* (a keyword, `abilities/keywords.ts`): as the fight begins its side draws x cards (`battle/tarot.ts`) and picks one in secret; the AI picks by a rough worth (task odds × reward). Tasks, named for the arcana: *Death* (kill an enemy before round 2 or 3 ends), *The Tower* (one hit of 1.2× or 1.5× the side's hardest hitter, rounded to 5), *Strength* (lose no one before round 3 or 4 ends), *Judgement* (kill the enemy with the most max HP), *Temperance* (heal your side 15% of its total max HP), *Wheel of Fortune* (two kills in one round). Rewards, to the whole side or every enemy: +15 or 25% damage for the fight, heal 20 or 30% of max HP, −5 or 10 enemy armor for the fight, or 15 or 25 damage to every enemy. A hand's tasks differ; rewards are drawn freely. The other side sees which card was picked (its name) but never its task or reward (the user, 2026-10-05: "more mysterious that way"); failed cards pay nothing. Each arcana stands for one kind of task, so a player who learns the deck can guess the kind, not the numbers or the reward. No unit has Tarot yet.
