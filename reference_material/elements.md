# Elements, as they look

The user, 2026-10-10, after night one's painted HUD came out grey and bland: "maybe it makes sense to actually just
write down similar descriptions of what items you see on the images honestly. Because maybe that can make you find
the details. Write down what you see, the actual elements." And: "it's descriptions we'll use to generate in the end
anyway."

So this file is not about lessons (those are in the `notes.md` files and `docs/design/hud-references.md`). It is a
catalogue of single elements, each written the way an image prompt needs it:
- **object**: what it is, in one noun
- **form**: silhouette, proportions, parts
- **material**: what it is made of, as specifically as the picture allows
- **colour**: the actual hues, including the small accents
- **finish**: polished, worn, tarnished, lacquered, cracked, grimy, glossy
- **ornament**: what is carved, cast, set or painted on it
- **joinery**: how it meets its neighbours (rivets, studs, clasps, overlaps)
- **light**: where its light comes from, and whether anything glows

These describe other games' pieces. They are a vocabulary to draw from, never prompts to copy: no screenshot goes to
an image generator, and no signature piece is transcribed (memory: references-are-direction). The section at the end
turns the vocabulary into what disc's pieces could be made of.

## Disciples II

**The map's command column** (`d2-detail-map-panel-2x.png`)
- *Column body.* Object: a full-height vertical housing, one fifth of the screen wide. Material: dark gunmetal iron cast
  in openwork, with deep ribbed fans (like the gills of a mushroom, or organ pipes radiating) behind each round
  cluster. Colour: charcoal to warm grey-brown, never blue-grey; the iron has a faint olive-bronze cast in its
  highlights. Finish: worn, grimy in the recesses, highlights rubbed pale on the raised edges. Light: from the upper
  left, strong enough that every raised rim has a pale edge and a black shadow.
- *Round buttons.* Object: coin-sized discs in deep iron sockets. Material: bone-white enamel or ivory face with a
  black engraved glyph (quill, open book, framed picture, handshake, towers); the socket a thick black-iron ring.
  Unavailable ones: the same socket, the face dark slate with no glyph.
- *Joint studs.* Object: small square studs set on the diagonal (diamonds), at every seam. Material: amber-gold
  faceted glass or polished brass with a bright point highlight. Colour: honey to orange-gold. The one warm colour on
  the column.
- *Movement plaque.* Object: a rectangle beside the portrait. Material: pale veined marble, cream-grey with darker
  grey veins, like a Carrara offcut. Text: black serif, centred.
- *Name strip.* Object: a thin band under the portrait. Material: the same marble, a darker grey.
- *Day cartouche.* Object: a small oval with the day number. Material: pale stone with a raised rim, a black serif
  numeral.
- *Portrait well.* Object: a square window with the hero's painted face. Frame: thin black iron, no ornament; the
  portrait carries the colour (rust-orange beard, horned helm).
- *Minimap well.* A black recess, its own rim the column's iron; the map inside is the only saturated thing on the
  column (orange, teal, white).

**The command wheel and its atlantes** (`d2-detail-command-wheel-atlantes-3x.png`)
- *Wheel.* Object: a round iron disc with seven button sockets in a ring around a centre. Material: black iron, the
  sockets sunk deep with thick bevelled rims. Behind it a half-sunburst of radiating iron ribs.
- *Centre orb.* Object: a sphere the size of one button. Material: dark green glass with a swirl or nebula inside and
  one white specular point. Colour: bottle green to near black. The only coloured light on the wheel.
- *Atlantes.* Object: two nude human figures, crouching, one at each side, arms reaching over the top of the wheel as
  if bracing it. Material: pale weathered stone, warm beige-grey, a little lighter than the iron, smooth. They are cut
  off by the column's edges; the wheel covers their forearms.
- *Header slot above.* A narrow recessed slot of pale marble, with a frieze of tiny carved figures in the iron band
  below it.

**The battle's bottom bar** (`d2-detail-battle-bottom-bar-2x.png`)
- *Portrait rings.* Object: two big round portholes, one at each end (your unit left, the target right), a quarter of
  the bar's length together. Material: thick dark iron ring with a raised inner lip; the portrait fills it to the
  edge. Colour: the portraits are the loudest colours on the screen (red eyes, a green dragon on rust-orange).
- *Name plaques.* Object: long tapered plaques flowing out of each ring towards the centre. Material: pale cream
  parchment-marble, mottled. Text: "Anti-Paladin / HP : 146/220", black serif. Ends: curled iron brackets.
- *Iron filigree.* Thorny wrought-iron scrolls grow along the top edge out of the rings, curling into the scene;
  black iron, thin, like briars or bramble.
- *Centre cluster.* Object: five round buttons in a cross under a gothic tracery arch. Material: black-iron sockets,
  slate faces with pale engraved glyphs (flag, shield, hourglass, crossed swords); the two active ones ivory.
  Small green glass inlays in the arch.
- *Orb slots.* Two pale moonstone-white glass orbs in diamond-shaped iron settings, set on the diagonal near the
  right ring.
- *Studs.* The same amber diamond studs along the bottom edge.

**The battle's grid panel** (`d2-battle-1.png`)

  bottom of the spine; iron filigree spilling over the top-right corner.
- *Cells.* Square portraits; a cell under attack is washed flat bright red with the damage number in white; HP on a

**The capital** (`d2-capital-undead.png`)

**The hire-a-leader scroll** (`d2-hire-leader.jpg`)
- *Scroll.* Object: a tall parchment sheet down the middle of the screen. Material: pale tan paper with a faint
  fibrous grain, a Greek-key border in dark ink near the top. Bottom: a turned wooden roller, warm honey wood, with
  rounded knobs at both ends.
- *Seals.* Object: two wax seals on the roller's right end. Material: deep crimson wax, glossy, with a raised rim;
  one embossed with a check mark, one with an X.
- *Gold on the roller.* A small pile of gold coins with "76" in black serif, painted on the roller itself.
- *Portrait strip.* Five portraits stacked in a thin dark-iron film strip on the scroll's left edge, each a deep red
  or purple painting.
- *The city behind.* Black iron architecture: round porthole wells (one black, one blood-red with a demon tower),
  white marble panels, a quatrefoil lattice of iron below, triangular iron arrow buttons, small white studs.

**The unit card** (`d2-unit-info-undead.jpg`, `d2-portrait-frames-compare.jpg`)
- *Sheet.* Object: a parchment sheet hanging from a rod. Material: aged tan parchment, mottled, darker at the edges.
  Rod: dark turned wood with iron ball finials. Down both sides: black wrought-iron vines with thorns and curls.
- *Stat list.* Black serif, label left and value right, no icons.
- *Portrait frames, one per faction:*
  - Undead: pale tarnished silver, a skull at the top centre, curling hooks at the corners like bone, a pierced
    fan-shaped ornament at the bottom.
  - Empire: dark iron with a small lion head at the top, a simple bevelled rim.
  - Possessed: no frame at all; the portrait shows through a ragged hole torn or burned in the parchment, edged in
    dark dried-blood red.
  - Neutral: a copper-brown frame whose crest is a horned skull with wings.
  - A darker variant: black iron with a lion-head crest and a beaded rim (fan extraction).

**The options screen** (`d2-options.png`)
- *Rows.* Long marble plaques, pale cream-grey with soft grey veining, black serif labels.
- *Toggles.* Object: an oval gem at the row's right end. Material: faceted green glass. On: lit from inside, bright
  leaf green with white highlights, crackled like shattered glass. Off: the same gem dark bottle green, unlit.
- *Value selectors.* A plaque between two triangular iron arrows (black iron, pale bevel).
- *Spine.* A vertical iron spine between the two columns, set with long green crystal lozenges.
- *Headers.* Small marble plaques with heavier black serif ("Game settings", "Audio").
- *Background.* Black iron lattice of quatrefoils, crosses and pointed arches.
- *Title.* "Options" on a marble plaque at the top centre in italic serif, set into the frame's top edge.

**The main menu** (`d2-main-menu.png`)
- *Post.* A tall black iron lattice post, visible only between the plaques.
- *Plaques.* Seven long horizontal plaques, each with arrowhead-pointed ends. Material: pale marble or stone, cream
  with soft grey mottling, a thin black iron border. Each end cap holds a small cut teal gem (aquamarine) in a black
  setting, like an eye. Text: black serif capitals, centred.
- *Painting.* Twisted dark trees, a stormy sky with a pale break of light, yellowed grass, a river; a skull and a gold
  coin in the grass; a dagger as the cursor.

**The spell book** (`d2-spellbook.png`)
- *Book.* An open book lying flat and square to the viewer (not at an angle), filling the screen. Pages: warm cream
  parchment with ragged deckled edges and brown foxing; cover: dark leather showing at the edges.
- *Spell icons.* Hand-drawn runes in red or blue ink on small parchment tiles, each in a thin ink border.
- *Bookmarks.* Five cloth ribbons hanging from the bottom edge, royal blue with red borders and swallowtail ends,
  Roman numerals I–V in white.
- *Mana crystals.* A row of five crystal shards (black, white, blue, red, green), each with its number below.
- *Props on the table.* Gold coins and a green glass bottle at the left edge, a quill at the right. The table: warm
  brown wood planks.
- *Text.* Black serif on the paper, the selected entry in a thin black rule box.

**The race select** (`d2-race-select-all.jpg`)
- *Monuments, one per race, each a different material and colour grade:*
  - Empire: a carved grey-stone pillar shaped like a sword with spiked crossguard flanges, a cut ice-blue gem set
    at its top; knotwork panels down its shaft; a green meadow with white flowers, a blue-grey storm sky.
  - Mountain Clans: a single dark rock spire with a carved knot-rune on its face, in a sea of snow and ice; a
    shaft of blue light.
  - Legions: a curved black horn of rock in a field of red; the sky and the ground glow ember red and black.
  - Undead: a black wooden shrine on stilts with skulls hung at its eaves and spikes at its corners; sepia,
    ochre-yellow sky, dead trees.
  - Elves: a tall crystal glowing acid-green from inside, gripped at its base by black twisted roots, in a warm
    sepia forest gorge with a low sun.
- *Selector.* A "Choose Race" marble plaque in italic serif over a long black-iron bar; on the bar, cut blue gems
  (sapphire) in diamond settings and two teal gem arrows at the ends; the race's name on a marble plate in the
  middle.

**The carry-over screen** (`d2-carryover-items.png`)
- *Spine.* A vertical black-iron spine down the middle with a horizontal arm across: pale aqua crystals (like
  aquamarine or ice) set along both, small ones at the ends, growing to one large faceted crystal in a pointed
  diamond setting at the crossing.
- *Lattice.* The whole background is black iron cast as gothic tracery: quatrefoils, pointed arches, circles with

- *Item sockets.* Round iron lattice wells, each a circle with a diamond cut in it, holding the item (a red banner,

- *Item rows.* An item in a square iron well, its text on a marble plaque beside it.
- *Portraits.* Round porthole wells at the top corners, one filled (a purple-and-green demon lord), one empty: a

- *Arrows.* Pale stone triangles in square iron sockets, for scrolling.

**The hero's equipment** (`d2-hero-inventory.jpg`)

- *Slots around him.* Diamond-shaped iron lattice windows set on the diagonal, each with an item: a red-and-gold
  banner, a purple orb, a sword, a necklace of small skulls, an amber gem.
- *Squad portraits.* A column of square portraits (purple, black, red demons) with the HP on small pale plaques

- *Ability list.* A marble plaque with small coloured icons per line: a scroll, a blue ring, a banner, a brown
  book, a blue orb, a red talisman.
- *Divider column.* A black iron spine down the middle carved with pointed arches and bosses; the button cluster

**Diplomacy** (`d2-diplomacy.png`)
- *Wells.* Large round porthole wells sunk in black stone, each holding a lord's face in strong colour (a blue-skinned

## Icewind Dale, Baldur's Gate, Planescape, Pillars

**IWD1's bottom wall** (`iwd1-hud-1.png`)
- *Wall.* Object: the strip under the view, painted as a stretch of rough dark stone with gnarled black roots crawling
  across it. Colour: cold grey-green stone, near-black roots, a little moss olive. Finish: damp, grimy.
- *Log slot.* A long black recess cut into the wall; at its right end a small gold double-spindle knob (turned brass,
  like a bedpost finial) as the scroll control.
- *Action buttons.* Object: square tiles. Material: warm reddish-brown wood, each a raised frame around a recessed
  square. Glyphs: carved relief in ivory-bone (a heraldic shield with a red cross, a bald head speaking, a sword,
  crossed weapons, pip patterns for formations). The wood is the warm, touchable material against cold stone.
- *The orb.* Object: a glass sphere sunk where the left column meets the bottom bar. Material: clear glass lit
  greenish-gold from inside, small white skulls visible through it, a dark iron ring around it. The brightest object
  on the HUD.
- *Left column.* Square wooden tiles on riveted dark planks, each icon a bone-coloured relief: a teal diamond emblem
  at the top, a quill, armour, a bust, a book, a scroll, a hand.
- *Portraits.* Six painted faces in a stone column, each with only a thin outline (green when selected) and the HP in
  small white numerals on the paint.
- *Rest button.* A wide wooden plank with a carved closed eye, its lashes in relief.

**IWD1's inventory** (`iwd1-inventory-1.png`)

## What the catalogue says, read across it

**The materials in these pictures.** Counted from the descriptions above, these are what the references are made of.
Grey stone is one entry among many, and it is never alone:
- *Metals:* black wrought iron, gunmetal, pewter, polished brass, tarnished brass, dark bronze, cast bronze, gilt,
  tarnished silver, copper, verdigris, rusted iron with red-brown edges, steel bosses.
- *Stones:* cracked flagstone, Carrara-like veined marble, teal veined marble, pale limestone, mossy olive stone,
  sandstone, slate, blue-grey slate, black basalt, warm sandstone blocks.
- *Glass and gems:* amber faceted studs, aquamarine and teal crystals, sapphire, moonstone, bottle-green glass, a
  green nebula orb, smoky scrying glass, crimson and navy glass orbs, stained glass, a Tiffany shade, cobalt glass.
- *Organic:* parchment (tan, cream, foxed, deckled), turned honey wood, mahogany, dark planks, oxblood leather, red
  marbled leather, brown marbled leather, crimson wax, cloth ribbons (royal blue, red), black banner cloth, rawhide,
  bone, ivory, tusk, bark, leaves, roots, moss.
- *Liquids and light:* blood in vials, mana liquid, candle and torch flame, inner glow of orbs and gems, bead lights.

**The colour, every time.** Each reference has a dark main material and then at least two accents that are small,
saturated and repeated: Disciples II's amber studs and green glass; IWD2's teal-and-rust mosaic; Diablo's red and blue
glass; Total War's polished brass; Darkest Dungeon's gold blackletter and red; Heroes III's red leather and gold rule;
Blood Omen's teal marble and red. The paintings (portraits, cities, icons) carry the strongest colour, but the chrome
is never colourless.

**Who stands where, counted.** Figures per screen: Disciples II one pair (the atlantes) or none; IWD2 one statue and
two tiny shield-bearers; Diablo two; Total War two different end figures; Darkest Dungeon one mirrored relief; Heroes
III none. Most screens have **no figure at all**: Disciples II's options, main menu, hire scroll, spell book, carry-over
screen; IWD1's inventory and menu; Heroes III's town and mage guild; Pillars, Bloodborne, Grim Dawn's map. Their
richness comes from materials, objects and the paintings.

**Objects instead of figures.** What makes a reference screen feel crafted, most often, is a *thing*: a wax seal, a
turned roller, a bookmark ribbon, a candle, a chest, a scroll tied with red cord, a brass telescope, a satchel, a heap
of coins, a torch sconce, a boarded-up slot, chains in an X, a glass vial, a lit gem, a pocket watch.

**Flat to the viewer.** Every piece that carries content faces the player square: the spell book lies flat and square,
the scrolls unroll straight, the slabs stand upright. Nothing that holds text is turned at an angle.

## How night one's HUD came out grey, traced through the prompts

Questioning each step, in order:
1. **The material line was one material.** Every probe's prompt ended with the same line: "Black wrought iron and dark
   stone like an old reliquary, worn smooth, with tarnished silver edges and deep shadows. Desaturated, grim and
   solemn." That names two dark greys and a third grey metal, and then asks for *desaturated*. No reference in the
   catalogue above is described that way. Disciples II's iron has amber studs and green glass; its marble is cream; its
   parchment is tan; its seals are crimson.
2. **"The chrome stays grey" was a misreading.** `hud-kit.md` wrote: "Paintings ... carry the colour, so the chrome
   stays grey (Disciples II)". The Disciples notes said the chrome is *neutral* and the *portraits* carry the colour. In
   the pictures, "neutral" is iron plus cream marble plus tan parchment plus honey wood plus amber, teal and green
   gems. It got shortened to grey, and grey went into every prompt.
3. **Marble and parchment never reached the painting.** The kit has five materials (stone, iron, marble, parchment,
   light), but the subject lines asked only for "dark stonework", "carved black stone", "dark stone sill". The marble
   plate and the panels came out grey because the words for them were.
4. **The figures were the subject, the materials an afterthought.** Every subject named its angel in detail and its
   surfaces in two words. In the references, figures are rare and the materials are described in depth.
5. **One painting meant one light and one palette.** Painting the whole screen at once gave every piece the same
   material in the same light. The references' pieces each have their own: the parchment is warm, the iron cool, the
   gem lit from inside.
6. **The steles came from "stele".** The subject line asked for "a tall stone stele" twice, then the title screen
   asked for a stele again. Hence the tombstones.
7. **The angles came from the generator, unchecked.** Nothing in the prompts asked for flat, square-on content
   surfaces, and the book and the angel came out turned.

## Words for disc's pieces, from the catalogue

Not decisions; a vocabulary to write prompts from. Faction motifs stay provisional (#76) and the user overrules.
- *Joinery that has colour:* amber faceted studs at the seams; small cut garnets in iron settings; brass rivets; a
  thin lead line with red glass between pieces (Jilliath's stained glass, from #76).
- *Plates that read:* cream veined marble with a fine black-iron rim; tan parchment, deckled, foxed at the edges;
  oxblood leather with gold tooling for headers.
- *Objects with jobs:* a crimson wax seal to confirm, a turned honey-wood roller with the gold on it, bookmark ribbons
  for tabs, a lit candle for the selected thing, a hand bell for End turn, an hourglass on a chain for the round, a
  reliquary vial of red for health, a wax-sealed iron band for locks, a velvet-lined tray for the sockets.
- *Metals for warmth:* tarnished brass and dark gilt against the iron, so not every piece is cold.
- *Glass for light:* red and amber stained glass lit from behind; a lamp, not a glow.
- *Wood for touch:* dark oak or walnut planks for the buttons the player presses, against the stone, the way IWD1
  sets warm wood into cold stone.
