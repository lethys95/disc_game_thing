# HUD reference material

Notes on every element of old gothic-fantasy interfaces, gathered 2026-10-09 for the UI kit
(`.kanban/in-progress/ui-kit-grotesques.md`). The lessons and the ideas for disc are in the repo:
`docs/design/hud-references.md`. Open `index.html` (served at `/reference_material/index.html`) to read every entry
next to its picture.

- `disciples/`: Sacred Lands, Disciples II (every race's capital, the map and battle HUD), III, Liberation
- `diablo/`: Diablo 1, 2 and Resurrected, 3, 4
- `infinity-engine/`: Icewind Dale 1 and 2, Baldur's Gate 1 and 2 and the Enhanced Editions, Planescape: Torment, Pillars of Eternity, Pathfinder
- `strategy/`: Warcraft III's four consoles, Heroes of Might and Magic III and V, Darkest Dungeon 1 and 2, Total War: Warhammer, King's Bounty, Age of Wonders
- `rpg/`: Path of Exile 1 and 2, Grim Dawn, Vampire: The Masquerade – Redemption, Arcanum, Divine Divinity, Blasphemous, Dungeon Keeper, Blood Omen

The notes and `ui-inventory.md` are committed. The screenshots they name are other games' art: they stay local
(gitignored), are never committed and are never fed to an image generator. `index.html` is generated locally from the notes.
